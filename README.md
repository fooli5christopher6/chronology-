            <p>The historical narrative of the Hebrew Bible is not a collection of isolated myths or unanchored folklore; it is a meticulously structured chronological continuum. From the primeval genealogical spans of Genesis to the monarchical collapses recorded in Kings and Chronicles, the biblical text embeds specific, numerical markers into every stage of redemptive history. <strong>The Chronological Matrix of Israelite History</strong> organizes this textual record into two comprehensive tables, mapping the patriarchal lifespans, early eras, heroic judge periods, and regnal durations down to the collapse of the Judean monarchy under Zedekiah.</p>

            <h2>1. The Primeval and Patriarchal Foundations: Creation to the Exodus Baseline</h2>
            <p>The first major division of the chronological matrix establishes the genealogical line stretching from Adam down to the entry into Egypt and the eventual Exodus. The primeval data preserved in <strong>Genesis 5</strong> and <strong>Genesis 11</strong> records the age of each patriarch at the birth of his designated heir, forming a continuous, mathematically verifiable chain of human history.</p>

            <pre><code>[ Antediluvian Line ] ──▶ [ Postdiluvian Line ] ──▶ [ Patriarchal Era ] ──▶ [ Exodus Anchor ]
(Adam to Noah: Gen 5)     (Shem to Terah: Gen 11)   (Abraham to Jacob)      (Exodus 12:40–41)</code></pre>

            <h3>A. The Antediluvian Generational Metrics (Genesis 5)</h3>
            <p>The antediluvian sequence begins with Adam at age <strong>130</strong> (<strong>Genesis 5:3</strong>) and proceeds through Seth (<strong>105y</strong>), Enosh (<strong>90y</strong>), Kenan (<strong>70y</strong>), Mahalalel (<strong>65y</strong>), Jared (<strong>162y</strong>), Enoch (<strong>65y</strong>), Methuselah (<strong>187y</strong>), Lamech (<strong>182y</strong>), and Noah at age <strong>500</strong> (<strong>Genesis 5:32</strong>). Far from being ornamental numbers, these ages function as precise chronological links that allow for the exact calculation of the antediluvian timeline up to the Deluge.</p>
